Home / Call for Papers

Submission Scope & Instructions

  • Original Manuscripts: Papers must describe original research not submitted or published elsewhere.
  • Formatting: Manuscripts must follow standard Springer CCIS/LNCS format (maximum 6–8 pages including references).
  • Review Process: Double-blind peer review handled through Microsoft CMT.
  • Proceedings: Accepted and registered papers will be published in Springer LNAI / CCIS (Scopus Indexed).
