About Budge Budge Institute of Technology (BBIT)

A Premier Engineering & Management Institute in Kolkata, West Bengal.

Nurturing Global Innovators Since 2009

Established in 2009 under the visionary leadership of the Jagannath Gupta Family Trust (JB Group), Budge Budge Institute of Technology (BBIT) stands as a landmark for technical higher education in South Kolkata.

Approved by AICTE, New Delhi, affiliated with Maulana Abul Kalam Azad University of Technology (MAKAUT), and accredited with a B++ Grade by NAAC as well as NBA, BBIT features state-of-the-art computing laboratories, modern IoT and robotics centers, high-speed campus internet, and extensive placement footprints in premier MNCs.

Scope & Submissions

Conference Tracks (5 Tracks & 23 Topics)

Submit original manuscripts addressing any of the following research tracks for QINS-2026.

Track 1

Quantum Computing and Quantum Algorithms

  • Variational Quantum Algorithms for Optimization (VQE, QAOA)
  • Quantum Machine Learning and Quantum Neural Networks
  • Fault-Tolerant Quantum Computing and Error Correction
  • Hybrid Quantum–Classical Computing Architectures
  • Quantum Algorithms for Graph and Combinatorial Problems
Track 2

Advanced Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning

  • Large Language Models and Vision-Language AI Systems
  • Self-Supervised and Representation Learning
  • Reinforcement Learning for Autonomous Systems
  • Autonomous Systems and Drone Networks
Track 3

Graph Theory, Game Theory and Network Science

  • Graph Neural Networks and Graph Representation Learning
  • Large-Scale Graph Mining and Network Analytics
  • Algorithmic Game Theory and Strategic Decision Systems
  • Dynamic and Temporal Graph Analysis
  • Graph Algorithms for Complex Network Optimization
Track 4

Distributed Systems, Edge Computing and Signal Processing

  • Distributed and Cloud-Native Computing Architectures
  • Edge AI and Intelligent Edge Computing Systems
  • Scalable Distributed Data Processing Frameworks
  • Signal Processing for Intelligent and Multimedia Systems
  • Signal Processing for IoT and Edge Devices
Track 5

Cryptography, Blockchain and Optimization for Secure Systems

  • Post-Quantum Cryptography and Secure Communication
  • Blockchain Systems and Distributed Ledger Technologies
  • Smart Contracts and Decentralized Applications
  • Privacy-Preserving Cryptographic Protocols

Campus Location & Connectivity

Venue & How to Reach BBIT

K.P. Mondal Road, Nischintapur, Budge Budge, Kolkata – 700137, West Bengal, India.

Air Connectivity (NSCBI Airport, CCU)

Netaji Subhash Chandra Bose International Airport (CCU), Kolkata is located approximately 38 km from BBIT campus. Pre-paid airport taxis and App Cabs (Uber / Ola) reach the campus in ~1 hour 15 minutes.

Rail Connectivity (Howrah / Sealdah / BGB)

• Budge Budge Railway Station (BGB): 3 km from campus (10 mins via auto/rickshaw).
• Sealdah Station (SDAH): 24 km.
• Howrah Junction (HWH): 26 km.

Campus Accommodation & Hotels

BBIT Executive Guest House rooms are available upon prior reservation for delegates. Luxury and business partner hotels are located along the Taratala and Alipore corridors.
