How to Reach BBIT

Campus Location & Connectivity

K.P. Mondal Road, Nischintapur, Budge Budge, Kolkata – 700137, West Bengal, India.

Air Connectivity (NSCBI Airport, CCU)

Netaji Subhash Chandra Bose International Airport (CCU), Kolkata is situated approximately 38 km from BBIT campus. Pre-paid airport yellow cabs, Uber, and Ola are available 24/7 directly to the campus via Maa Flyover / Taratala.

Railway Stations

• Budge Budge Station (BGB): 3 km from campus (frequent local trains from Sealdah South section).
• Sealdah Station (SDAH): 24 km.
• Howrah Junction (HWH): 26 km (connected via Vidyasagar Setu & Taratala).

Roadways & Public Transport

BBIT is located right off K.P. Mondal Road (Opposite IOC Bottling Plant). Regular AC/Non-AC public buses (Route 77, SD76, Budge Budge Minibuses) operate from Esplanade, Taratala, and Behala directly to Budge Budge.

VISA Assistance for Foreign Delegates

International delegates requiring an official Invitation Letter for Conference Visa application must complete their registration and send their accepted Paper ID, passport scan, and affiliation to icscca@bbit.edu.in. Official institutional clearance letters will be provided promptly.
