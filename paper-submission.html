Workflow

Submission & Review Lifecycle

1

Manuscript Prep

Format paper using IEEE A4 two-column templates (Max 6 pages).

2

CMT Upload

Submit anonymized PDF to Microsoft CMT portal selecting relevant BBIT track.

3

Double-Blind Review

Minimum 2-3 independent peer reviews per paper.

4

Camera-Ready

Incorporate reviewer revisions, check IEEE PDF eXpress and eCF.

Plagiarism & Ethics Policy

All submissions will be rigorously screened with Turnitin. Papers with similarity exceeding 15% overall (or >3% single source) will be rejected without review.

IEEE No-Show Policy

Papers that are accepted must be registered and presented by an author at BBIT campus or online. Unpresented papers will be removed from IEEE Xplore.
